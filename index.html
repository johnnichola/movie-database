What is MovieDB?

MovieDB is an IDT built movie and TV database. Every piece of data is from an API called The Movie Database (TMBb). MovieDB's strong international focus and breadth of data is largely unmatched and something we're incredibly proud of. Put simply, we live and breathe community and that's precisely what makes us different.

Why Choose MovieDB?

  • Browse movies and TV shows without creating an account, entering payment details, or installing an app.
  • Find movies, new releases, top IMDb titles, classic, and popular films in one place.
  • Explore TV series pages with seasons, episodes, and title details.
  • Browse on phones, tablets, laptops, desktops, and smart TV browsers.
  • Browse new releases, trending movies, popular TV shows, and fresh title pages.
